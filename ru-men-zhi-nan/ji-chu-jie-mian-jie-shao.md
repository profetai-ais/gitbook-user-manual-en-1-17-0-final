### Quick edit and settings page

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (96).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (97).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The edit button in a list opens **quick edit**, which offers only common fields such as name and description. The window title shows "Edit (item name)", so you can confirm which item you are editing.

For full settings, click the item's name to open its settings page. If the item has an enable switch, you can also switch its enabled state on that page.

## Side menu

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (92).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The side menu is divided into groups by function, and only one group is expanded at a time:

* When you open another group, the group that was expanded collapses automatically.
* When you switch pages, the system automatically expands the group the current page belongs to.
* If you collapse a group manually, it is not expanded again while you switch between pages within that same group.

## Notification messages

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (91).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

When you save or delete something, or when an error occurs, a notification message appears:

* Messages appear at the top center of the screen and slide in from the top. When several appear at once, they stack in order.
* Messages disappear automatically after 5 seconds; error messages disappear after 10 seconds. Hovering over a message pauses the countdown, which resumes when you move the pointer away.
* Messages other than errors (such as information and warnings) have an "×" in the top-right corner. Click it to close the message immediately.
* Error messages do not have an "×". Click "Ask Copilot" on the message to ask about the cause of the error. For details, see [Asking Copilot when an error occurs](../ping-tai-zhi-nan/ai-studio-copilot.md#yu-dao-cuo-wu-shi-xun-wen-copilot).

## Time format

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (95).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Modules in the Analytics group (such as Job Management, Report, Audit Log and Workforce Monitor) always show times in `YYYY/MM/DD HH:MM:SS` format.

## Display language

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (221).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

1. Click the **Preferences** icon in the panel on the right side of the screen.
2. On the Preferences page, click the supported languages **drop-down menu**.
3. Under **Display Language**, select the interface language you want (for example: English / Traditional Chinese / Simplified Chinese / Japanese / Thai).

> Note: In the Thai interface, Agent and Token are still shown in English for now.
